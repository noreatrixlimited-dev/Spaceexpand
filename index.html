How to play

PaintDrag anywhere to carve on your board. Leave your land to draw a trail, then come back home to claim everything inside the loop.
ThrowTap (or the ball button) to throw. Splats paint land, cut enemy trails and stun players. More land = more balls!
Careful!If anyone touches your trail, you get bonked and your egg loses a heart.
StealTouch enemy eggs to pop their hearts. With 0 hearts, grab the egg and ride back onto your own colour to keep it. Bump a rider carrying an egg to send it home.
SurviveAfter 1:00 the clouds eat the island's edge. Grab the star for power-ups. If your nest is captured, you're out!

Phone: drag to steer, tap to throw. Keyboard: WASD / arrows to steer, Space to throw.

Win by being the last nest standing, or own the most land when time runs out.
