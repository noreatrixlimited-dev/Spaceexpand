How to play

PaintLeave your land to draw a flower trail. Come back home to claim everything inside the loop.
Careful!If anyone touches your trail, you get bonked and your egg loses a heart.
StealTouch enemy eggs to pop their hearts. With 0 hearts, grab the egg and carry it to your nest. Bump a thief to send an egg home.
SurviveAfter 1:00 the clouds eat the island's edge. Grab the star for power-ups. Captured? You're a ghost: touch another nest to break free!

Swipe or use arrow keys / WASD to steer.

Win by being the last nest standing, or own the most land when time runs out.
